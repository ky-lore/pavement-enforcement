Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains how Pavement Enforcement ("we", "us") handles information you give us through pavementenforcement.com, by phone or by text message.

What we collect

  • Information you enter in our forms: your name, phone number, email address, property address or ZIP code, the service you need and any message you write.
  • Information from calls and texts with us: your phone number, the time of the call or message, and what you tell us about your project.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, schedule site inspections, prepare estimates, do the work, send updates about your project and improve our website and service. We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages (SMS)

If you agree to receive text messages from Pavement Enforcement, we may text you about your inquiry, estimates, scheduling and service updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase. Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run our business (for example, website hosting, phone and messaging services, and customer-management tools), and only as needed to provide our services, or when required by law.

Cookies and analytics

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. We may use analytics and advertising tools, such as Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Google Ads, that use cookies to measure how visitors use the site and how well our ads work. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
