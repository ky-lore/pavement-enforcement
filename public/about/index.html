Who we are

We're Pavement Enforcement. We help property owners protect their investment by keeping their pavement in great shape. From sealcoating and striping to crack sealing and asphalt repairs, we focus on quality work, honest communication, and doing things the right way.

We want every customer to know they're getting a company they can trust, not just for one job, but for years to come.

Whether your pavement needs a simple repair, preventative maintenance or a full replacement, we'll recommend the solution that's right for your property, not just the one with the biggest price tag.

Pavement Enforcement is led by owner Diego Arce and is licensed, bonded and insured in California under CSLB License #1162987.

Standard Procedure

Inspect. Assess. Restore.

Every job follows the same four steps, so you always know what happens next.

01Step 01

Request an Inspection

Tell us about the property and what needs attention. We'll schedule a site visit and gather the information we need.

02Step 02

Inspect the Property

We walk the site, take measurements and photos, and identify the actual pavement conditions and problem areas.

03Step 03

Review Your Options

You receive a clear scope with practical recommendations based on pavement condition, budget and long-term goals.

04Step 04

Schedule the Work

We coordinate access, phasing and scheduling to reduce disruption to tenants, customers, employees and normal property operations.
